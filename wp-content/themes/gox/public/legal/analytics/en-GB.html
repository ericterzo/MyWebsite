IONOS SiteAnalytics

This service allows us to analyse user behaviour on our website to improve its functionality and user experience. IONOS SiteAnalytics helps us understand how visitors interact with our content, which pages are most popular, and how we can optimise our site's technical performance.

Data Purposes

This list represents the purposes of the data collection and processing.

  • Statistical analysis of user behaviour
  • Measurement of website reach
  • Technical optimisation of the website

Technologies Used

This service uses a tracking pixel or server-side log files to collect data. It does not use cookies to store information on your device.

Data Collected

This list represents all (personal) data that is collected by or through the use of this service.

  • Referrer (previously visited website)
  • Requested website or file
  • Browser type and browser version
  • Operating system used
  • Device type used
  • Time of access
  • IP address in anonymised form (used only to determine the location of access)

Legal Basis

Your consent, according to Art. 6(1)(a) of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Location of Processing

Germany and the European Union.

Retention Period

The collected data is stored for a period of 8 weeks and then automatically deleted.

Transfer to Third Countries

No data is transferred to countries outside the European Union.
