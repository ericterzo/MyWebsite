The first manufactured prototype is here!

After a couple of months of hard work finalising the rules, the designs and adapting everything to the manufacturers' templates, some shiny new components showed up at our door! This was all very exciting, and it made the following playtest sessions feel much more like a regular game night with a finished product; players were genuinely surprised by the components quality and smoothness of the gameplay.

The Player Board

The core of the game experience, players will start from just a house and workshop and build up their mining town.

The Day-Round board

This board was made to help players remember what happens every round.
Each round is structured like a day, with different things happening in each phase.

Worker hiring and  resource selling board

Specialists worker are drawn at random from a bag each day.

The resource market has a base cost at which you can sell resources, but if selling in groups, better deals can be obtained, but hurry! the slots for those special deals are limited each day!
